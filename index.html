FLAME INTELLIGENCE

AI-Powered Fire Safety Intelligence for Human Space Exploration.

Fire behaves differently without gravity: flames form spheres, burn at lower oxygen, and produce smoke that standard detectors can miss. This platform pulls NASA's microgravity fire research into one searchable place, ranks what matters for Moon and Mars missions, and lets you ask questions that are answered from cited sources.

How the evidence is graded

Every record is tagged as a primary NASA or peer-reviewed source, a secondary (news) source, team notes to verify, or a plan with no results.

All records come from the sources collected for this project. Records marked "Unchecked lead", "Team notes" or "Secondary source" should be checked before you cite them. Judgement scores on the Mission Insights page are the project team's, not NASA's.
